Code & tools

Pipelines and statistical methods built for terabyte-scale whole-brain imaging. Repositories live on github.com/kenjp1223.

Whole-brain preprocessing pipeline

End-to-end processing of light-sheet volumes from raw acquisition to registered, quantified cell counts: destriping and stitching, ClearMap-based cell detection, ANTs registration to the Allen CCFv3 and Kim Lab atlases, and per-region density extraction. Runs on HPC clusters at terabyte scale.

Hierarchical multiple-comparison framework

Brain-wide ensemble detection with a hierarchical testing procedure that exploits the nested structure of brain anatomy, improving detection power in small and sparsely activated regions where flat region-by-region correction fails. Developed with statistical collaborator Daniel Kessler.

Activity-to-gene-expression analysis

Links regional c-Fos activity to molecular identity by cross-referencing whole-brain activity maps against transcriptomic atlases, recovering cell-type markers and aligning opioid receptor expression with condition-specific activity patterns.

Teaching these methods

I have co-organized four annual hands-on workshops at the UW Center for the Neurobiology of Addiction, Pain, and Emotion, which have trained researchers from across departments.

  • Light Sheet Imaging Workshop 2024 — tissue clearing with the LifeCanvas SmartBatch system and the HPC preprocessing pipeline.
  • Imaging & Genetics Workshop 2021–2023 — virus injection, GRIN lens, and headcap implantation surgery. Co-organized all three; helped launch the series in 2021.
